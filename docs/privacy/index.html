Privacy Policy

PCSO Lotto Results & Analysis

Effective: 10 August 2026 Publisher: Kanonsoft

This app is independently developed and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by the Philippine Charity Sweepstakes Office (PCSO).

This policy explains how Kanonsoft handles information in PCSO Live Lotto Results and Analysis (the “App”), including information handled by services used for results, advertising, and purchases.

Information stored on your device

The App may keep the following in its private local storage:

  • Saved picks, selected games, draw slots, play modes, and combinations.
  • Appearance, reminder, and game-display preferences.
  • Cached public lottery results and the last successful refresh time.
  • A cached indication that the lifetime Remove Ads product is owned.
  • Advertising consent choices managed by Google’s consent SDK.
  • Local reminder schedules managed by the operating system.

The App does not create a Kanonsoft user account. Saved picks and app preferences are not intentionally sent to a Kanonsoft-operated server.

Lottery-result requests

The App connects to the LottoMatik result service at lottomatik.pcso.gov.ph to retrieve published lottery results. It may check on first launch, during later launches, after returning to the foreground, and around scheduled publication times.

Requests include the requested game and paging details. Like ordinary internet requests, the service and network providers may receive an IP address, request time, and connection metadata. Saved picks and entered combinations are not included in these requests.

Advertising and consent

Unless a verified Remove Ads purchase is active, the App uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to display advertisements and manage privacy choices.

Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share:

  • An IP address, which may be used to estimate general location.
  • App and ad interactions such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostic and SDK performance information.
  • Device and account identifiers, including advertising and app-set identifiers where available.

Google uses this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Information sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Ads may be personalized or non-personalized depending on consent, region, device settings, and advertising configuration.

A verified Remove Ads purchase prevents future ad requests from the App, but cannot recall information sent by an advertisement that was already loaded. See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps using its services.

Remove Ads purchase

Google Play processes the optional one-time lifetime Remove Ads purchase. The App receives product, localized price, purchase-state, purchase-token, and entitlement information needed to complete and restore it. The App does not receive full card, bank, or wallet details and does not operate its own payment processor. This product is not a subscription.

Notifications

When result reminders are enabled, the App requests notification permission and schedules local reminders at 3 PM, 5 PM, and 9 PM. The current App does not obtain a push token and does not use a Kanonsoft-operated notification server. Reminders can be disabled in the App or device settings.

Sharing

“Share this app” opens the device’s standard share sheet only after the user selects it. The chosen destination app handles shared information under its own privacy policy.

Access the App does not request

The App does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photo-library access. It has no separate Kanonsoft-operated analytics, account, or crash-reporting service. Google Mobile Ads still handles the advertising and diagnostic information described above while ads are enabled.

How information is used

  • Display and cache lottery results.
  • Save picks and preferences on the device.
  • Schedule requested reminders.
  • Display and measure advertisements.
  • Process and restore the Remove Ads entitlement.
  • Support security and fraud prevention performed by Google.

Retention and deletion

Local data remains until it is deleted, app storage is cleared, or the App is uninstalled. Individual picks can be edited or deleted inside the App. Because Kanonsoft does not operate an account or backend for saved picks, it cannot remotely retrieve or delete those local records. Google, LottoMatik, network providers, and share-sheet destinations retain information under their own policies.

Security

Network requests use HTTPS, and local records are kept in the App’s private device storage. No transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

  • Edit or delete saved picks.
  • Disable reminders or notification permission.
  • Manage advertising choices when Google’s privacy control is required.
  • Reset or delete the Android advertising ID in device settings.
  • Clear all local App data or uninstall the App.
  • Purchase Remove Ads or restore an existing Google Play purchase.

Children

The App is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not directed to children. Kanonsoft does not knowingly request names, contact details, or account information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when App features, service providers, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are published.

Contact

For a privacy inquiry, open an issue in the project’s GitHub repository. Do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue. For a private inquiry after release, use the developer contact shown on the App’s Google Play listing.
